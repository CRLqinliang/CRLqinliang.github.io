# Literature figures

This directory contains only separately licensed third-party figure assets and their retrieval provenance. Source authors, figure numbers, and licensing are recorded in ../concept_visuals.json and ../FIGURES.md. These assets are **not** relicensed under the project's MIT license.

- W4, W5, W6: Wan and Harada (2016), CC BY 4.0.
- R7, R10: Roa and Suárez (2015), CC Attribution per the article.
- T1: Lin et al. (2023), arXiv:2303.12153v5, Fig. 1, CC BY-SA 4.0. Cropped from the PDF; the cropped asset remains CC BY-SA 4.0.

The fetcher only attempts these explicit sources, and records unavailable files rather than fabricating originals. Other paper and textbook figure references are source links.
