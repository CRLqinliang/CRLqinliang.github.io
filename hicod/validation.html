Published execution reports

Reports are emitted by GitHub Actions after execution. Missing reports remain unpublished. Historical results identify their source commit and do not automatically certify the current deployed version.

02 / PHYSICAL SCOPE

Geometric agreement is not dynamic feasibility

The published MuJoCo 4.0.1 MP4 is an actual kinematic replay. The new cross-check uses MuJoCo distances at identical finite configurations, compares clearance thresholds, FK and fixed-mode graphs, and inspects selected regrasp witness samples.

Still unverified: independent IK search, continuous collision certificates, actual gripper force closure, support/contact dynamics, and hardware execution. Regrasp support remains idealized.

03 / MODEL EXPERIMENT SCOPE

Open-weight model pilot

Use the same Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct model, deterministic generation, all six profiles and four public-test tasks (one per class). This is a real-model protocol and reasoning pilot, not evidence about frontier models or unseen-task generalization.

Malformed outputs and exhausted budgets are recorded as unresolved; no scripted policy substitutes for model responses. Public tasks may be contaminated. Publication-scale evaluation still needs a private holdout and repeated multi-model trials.
