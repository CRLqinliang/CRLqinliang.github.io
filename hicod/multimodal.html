V0.5 / MULTIMODAL SOLUTION-SPACE DEBUGGING

Understand mode transitions.

Can a regrasp recover connectivity when a fixed-attachment transfer has no sampled path? Choose supported stages, compare fixed and hybrid graphs, and inspect release, radial retreat, empty-arm transit, approach and reacquisition evidence.

This is an analytic geometric model, not dynamically verified regrasp. Support is an explicit ideal assumption. Collision, IK and transitions are checked at finite samples only. No sampled path is not proof of continuous infeasibility.
